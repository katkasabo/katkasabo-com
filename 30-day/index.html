Day 01 Wednesday, 2 September 2026 # 100 words

The rule is that it gets posted

For years I have written things that never left the drafts folder. Not because they were bad, but because they were not finished, and finished is a moving target I keep repainting further down the road.

So here is the deal. One hundred words. Every day. Thirty days. Posted before I decide whether it is good, because that decision has never once helped me. If it is thin, it is thin in public. If it is sharp, that is a bonus. The point is the habit, not the artefact. Day one is the easy one. Ask me on day nineteen.
